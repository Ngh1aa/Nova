## Audit of the previous build (rendered at 1440 and 390)

1. Two primary navigations at once on desktop: a black floating rail **and** a top segmented tab bar with the same destinations.
2. The safe-to-spend amount and transfer amounts rendered in a monospace fallback (`IBM Plex Mono` was referenced but never loaded).
3. Colour carried no meaning: every transaction, account, bill and KPI row had its own pastel background, so the one row that needs review did not stand out.
4. Duplicate large titles on mobile (`Cards` above `Cards`, `Savings` above `Savings`).
5. Highlight card was white with three radial gradients; the formula behind "safe to spend" was hidden behind an `i` button.
6. Type below readable size in many tiles (9–11 px), inconsistent radii (12/13/14/18/22/24/28/30 px).
7. `taste-check.cjs` on 17 screens × 2 widths: **59 errors, 224 warnings** (34 contrast, 14 " · " joins, thin highlight cards, wrapped short labels).

## Brand brief (level B: brand exists, no written guide)

- Mark: three rounded strokes on ink `#16232B` — mint `#A9E5D2`, lavender `#C8BDF1`, sky `#AAD3FF`.
- Voice: calm, precise, truthful about simulation.
- Platform: responsive web prototype following iOS 26 conventions; system typography is required by the existing contract.

## Rules this layer enforces

- One primary navigation per breakpoint: rail ≥1200, top tabs 768–1199, floating dock <768.
- Colour only for meaning: action blue, safe green, needs-review amber, negative red, and the three Horizon parts.
- Lists are rows separated by hairlines; the needs-review row is the only tinted row.
- One highlight card per screen (Home hero). Other emphasis is type size, not fill.
- All money uses tabular numerals in the product font.
