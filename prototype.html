Modern Daybook — interactive prototype

Money clarity before money movement.

Nova is a consumer-banking portfolio case built around one decision: what money is truly safe to spend next? The interface treats balance, upcoming commitments, savings and protection as one readable financial field instead of a collection of generic fintech cards.
