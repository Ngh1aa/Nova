Nova · Financial Workspace · interactive prototype

Know what is safe to spend before the next money decision.

Nova is a consumer-finance product concept centered on one forward-looking question: what money is actually safe to spend after known commitments, planned savings and a protected buffer? The product connects planning, transaction trust, transfers, cards, savings and recovery into one decision model instead of a generic dashboard of balances.

Three product decisions worth reviewing

Decision 01 · planning model

Selected: Money Horizon

A forward-looking 14-day view combines known commitments, planned savings and protected buffer with the current balance.

Rejected direction: the retired ledger/daybook metaphor became visually dominant and made the product feel old, narrow and less like a current financial workspace.

Decision 02 · protection

Evidence before destructive action

An unusual payment opens transaction facts and review context before the user freezes a card. The freeze confirmation explains that already-authorized or offline payments may still settle.

Trade-off: one extra review step is accepted to reduce panic-driven action and make the consequence legible.

Decision 03 · money movement

Consequence before confirmation

Transfer review exposes recipient, amount, timing and projected remaining safe-to-spend before simulated authentication.

Trade-off: the flow is less “one-tap fast”, but the commitment is more inspectable and recovery keeps review context intact.
