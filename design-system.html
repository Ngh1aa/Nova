Motion & accessibility

Explain change

Horizon zones glide 600–800ms when commitments or contributions change. Small control feedback stays 120–240ms.

Never gamify money

No odometer values, confetti, glowing balances, parallax or scroll theatre.

Reduced motion

prefers-reduced-motion: reduce collapses non-essential motion while preserving all state information.

Focus

Keyboard focus uses a high-contrast blue ring distinct from success, risk and primary-action colors.

State redundancy

Status always combines text with shape/icon context; no high-consequence meaning is color-only.

System reality

Balances, KYC, risk signals, card controls, biometrics and transfers remain fictional/simulated.
