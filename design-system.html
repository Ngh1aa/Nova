Design system

Trust through restraint.

The system avoids the purple-gradient fintech shorthand. NOVA uses a warm neutral canvas, deep ink, restrained jade and semantic states that remain legible without relying on color alone.

Motion principles

Meaning first

Money Horizon zone widths glide when commitments or savings change. Motion explains cause and effect.

No casino behavior

No slot-machine number rolling, confetti, glowing balance pulses or celebratory money movement.

Reduced motion

prefers-reduced-motion: reduce collapses transitions to effectively instant updates while preserving all information.
