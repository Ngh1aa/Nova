Nova · Design system

Calm money, iOS clarity.

Nova combines dense financial information with soft iOS geometry: floating navigation, white elevated surfaces, pastel semantic groups and compact system typography. The goal is to make complex money decisions feel readable without turning the interface into a generic fintech card wall.

Visual signature

Rounded financial intelligence

Large-radius surfaces, compact nested controls and color-coded information groups create one recognizable system across Home, Activity, Pay, Cards and Save.

Shape rules

Primary cards use 28–32px radii, controls use about 18px, compact icon tiles use 14px, the floating black rail uses 30px and true pills use 999px. Borders stay subtle while elevation is reserved for major floating layers.

Motion & accessibility

Explain change

Large financial recomposition stays calm and deliberate. Small control feedback remains fast and tactile.

Never gamify money

No confetti, glowing balances or celebratory motion around spending and risk states.

Reduced motion

prefers-reduced-motion: reduce collapses non-essential motion while preserving all state information.

Focus

Keyboard focus uses a high-contrast blue ring distinct from success, risk and primary-action colors.

State redundancy

Status combines text with shape/context; no high-consequence meaning depends on color alone.

System reality

Balances, KYC, risk signals, card controls, biometrics and transfers remain fictional/simulated.
